About

I am a Senior Economist in the Economics Division of Ireland’s Department of Finance, on secondment from the Central Bank of Ireland. I hold a PhD in Economics from Trinity College Dublin.

My research is in applied macroeconomics, with interests in macroeconomic forecasting, monetary and financial economics, productivity and resource misallocation, and labour markets.
