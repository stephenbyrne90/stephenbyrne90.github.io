Explore Ireland’s monthly goods exports and imports by product and trading partner.

[Open the Irish Trade Monitor](https://dfin-economics.shinyapps.io/irish-trade-monitor/)

## How to use it

In the sidebar, choose **Exports** or **Imports**. Use **Headline year** and **Years to compare** to set the periods shown in the summary and comparison views.

Choose a tab based on what you want to find:

- **Overview** gives you the latest trade totals, main products and leading trading partners.
- **Monthly monitoring** lets you pick a month and see which products and partners drove changes.
- **Annual comparison** compares totals and the mix of products across years.
- **By country** lets you pick a trading partner and see which products Ireland trades with it.
- **Product by country** shows where Ireland exports a product or where it imports it from. **Product over time** shows how that product’s trade has changed month by month. In either view, search by product name or SITC code and select a row.

## Data

The monitor uses Eurostat Comext data (DS-059331). Values are in current euros. The latest one or two months are provisional and may be revised.
