The Irish Trade Monitor is an interactive application for exploring Ireland's merchandise trade data at a detailed product level.

Compare countries, products and time periods, and generate charts without downloading or manipulating the underlying data.

[Launch the Irish Trade Monitor](https://stephenbyrne-econ.shinyapps.io/irish-trade-monitor/)

*The dashboard opens in a separate application hosted on shinyapps.io.*
