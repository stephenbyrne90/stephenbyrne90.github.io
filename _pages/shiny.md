The **Irish Trade Monitor** is an interactive application for exploring Ireland's detailed merchandise trade data by product, partner and time period. Users can plot series and compare trade patterns without downloading the underlying datasets.

[Launch the Irish Trade Monitor](https://stephenbyrne-econ.shinyapps.io/irish-trade-monitor/)
