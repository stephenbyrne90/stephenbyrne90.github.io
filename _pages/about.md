I am a Senior Economist in the Economics Division of Ireland's Department of Finance, currently on secondment from the Central Bank of Ireland. My work spans macroeconomic research, forecasting and policy analysis. I hold a PhD in Economics from Trinity College Dublin.

My research interests are in applied macroeconomics, particularly monetary and financial economics, productivity and resource misallocation, and labour markets.

## Selected research

**[The Sources of Capital Misallocation in Europe](/publication/sources-of-capital-misallocation-europe)** (with Robert Goodhead, 2025)  
Central Bank of Ireland Research Technical Paper. We decompose the sources of capital misallocation across 19 European countries, distinguishing persistent distortions from adjustment frictions and transitory factors. [Paper (PDF)](https://www.centralbank.ie/docs/default-source/publications/research-technical-papers/the-sources-of-capital-misallocation-in-europe.pdf).

**[The evolution of household savings: determinants and implications](/publication/evolution-of-household-savings)** (with Laura Boyd and Tara McIndoe-Calder, 2025)  
Central Bank of Ireland Quarterly Bulletin signed article. We examine the structural factors behind higher household savings in Ireland and the changing composition of household wealth. [Paper (PDF)](https://www.centralbank.ie/docs/default-source/publications/quarterly-bulletins/quarterly-bulletin-signed-articles/the-evolution-of-household-savings-determinants-and-implications.pdf).

**[Intangible assets of multinational enterprises in Ireland and their impact on euro area activity](/publication/intangible-assets-mnes-ireland-euro-area-activity)** (with co-authors, 2024)  
ECB Occasional Paper No. 350. An analysis of how multinational enterprises' intangible assets affect measures of investment, output and trade in Ireland and the euro area. [Publication record](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/research/authors/profiles/stephen-byrne.et.html).
